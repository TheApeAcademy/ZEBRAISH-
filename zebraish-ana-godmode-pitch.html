The Board
Business Ecosystem
Where a launched product gets discovered and grows.
● Prototype
View full details ⌄
What it is

Not a feedback tool — a business ecosystem and discovery platform. Every company gets a "Drop": its own world inside The Board, with a community side and a signals side.

How it works
Hype = upvote Cap = downvote Receipt = structured feedback Drip = community post Going Flame = viral demand
For the business

Every complaint and request becomes ranked data — a Health Score, a flame-ranked wishlist, and a public status from Open to Built. Small companies get discovered the way people do on social media: on the strength of the thing itself, not the ad budget.
