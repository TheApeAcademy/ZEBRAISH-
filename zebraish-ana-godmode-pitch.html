02 — Why this needs to exist

Founders are stitching together five vendors to do one job.

Someone with a real idea today has to piece together a designer, a developer, a marketer, a feedback tool and an analytics dashboard — from five different companies who don't talk to each other — just to find out if anyone wants what they built.

05 — Prototype

The Board is where launched businesses go to be seen.

Not a feedback tool. A business ecosystem and discovery platform — every company gets a "Drop," its own world inside The Board, with a community side and a signals side. Customer complaints become bug reports. Feature requests become a ranked wishlist. Votes decide what gets built next, in public.

● Prototype — designed, not yet live
Discovery, built in
Social media let an unknown person go from nobody to somebody with one good post. The Board is built on the same idea, applied to businesses: a small company with something genuinely good shouldn't need a huge ad budget just to be noticed.
Drops (company pages) Hype / Cap voting What's Poppin feed
● Prototype — designed, not yet live
Signal → intelligence
Every complaint, request and bug report becomes structured data — ranked by demand, clustered by severity, rolled into a Health Score. Chaos in, structured truth out, for the business that owns the Drop.
Company dashboard Flame score ranking Status: Open → Built
